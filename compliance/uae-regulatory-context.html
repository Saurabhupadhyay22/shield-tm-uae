UAE regulatory context

Shield Astra is built to satisfy a specific regulatory landscape. This page gives you the map so the rest of the documentation makes sense — every feature is anchored to at least one of these frameworks, and every rule in the Rule Library cites the specific paragraph it implements.

The four bodies that matter​

Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) — regulates all licensed financial institutions, including PSPs under the Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes Regulation (RPSCS), and money-services businesses. CBUAE also publishes the UAE Local Terrorist List and hosts the goAML platform for STR/SAR filing.

Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (VARA) — the Dubai-based dedicated regulator for VASPs. Publishes the Compliance & Risk Management Rulebook, the Custody Services Rulebook, and the VA Broker-Dealer Rulebook, plus regime-specific rulebooks for advisory, lending, and staking services.

Financial Action Task Force (FATF) — the international standard-setter. CBUAE and VARA both align their rulebooks to FATF Recommendations. The UAE was on the FATF grey list from 2022 to 2024, exited in February 2024, and maintains an active FATF-alignment programme. Shield Astra's rule set is anchored on FATF Recommendations because that survives future regulatory churn.

UAE FIU (through the goAML platform) — the reporting endpoint for Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) and Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs). Every licensed FI files STRs to goAML.

CBUAE-specific frameworks​

For PSPs and other CBUAE-regulated FIs, the documents to know:

  • CBUAE AML/CFT Guidance for Licensed Financial Institutions — the umbrella guidance behind the UAE's implementation of FATF Recommendations
  • CBUAE RPSCS Regulation — the specific licensing regime for PSPs
  • CBUAE Notice 1362/2020 — obligations regarding the UAE Local Terrorist List; every LFI must screen against it in addition to FATF/UN/OFAC/EU/UK
  • CBUAE Notice 74/2019 — Suspicious Activity Report filing procedures
  • CBUAE Notice 6/2019 — Funds Transfer Statistics (FTS) reporting thresholds: AED 55,000 single transfer, aggregate reporting rules
  • CBUAE Guidance on AML/CFT for VASPs (August 2024) — CBUAE-side guidance specific to VASPs; complements VARA's regime

VARA-specific frameworks​

For VASPs:

  • VARA Compliance & Risk Management Rulebook v2 (February 2025) — the core CRMR; Part III covers customer records and the 8-year retention rule (exceeds FATF's 5)
  • VARA Custody Services Rulebook — Part IV.B.3 for wallet attribution proof (satoshi test / signed message requirement)
  • VARA VA Broker-Dealer Rulebook — per-customer LEI, source-of-wealth, distinct VA-specific risk score requirements
  • VARA Advisory Services Rulebook, VARA Lending Services Rulebook — activity-specific overlays

goAML — the STR/SAR filing endpoint​

goAML is a UN-developed platform that most FIU-Ns use, including UAE FIU. Every UAE-licensed FI must:

  1. Register on goAML (goaml.uaefiu.gov.ae) and obtain filing credentials
  2. Have an MLRO nominated as the goAML filer
  3. File STRs (Suspicious Transaction Reports) or SARs (Suspicious Activity Reports) via goAML for anything the compliance function determines is suspicious
  4. Retain the STR/SAR + supporting evidence for 5 years (FATF INR.11 minimum; VARA CRMR Part V mandates 8)

Shield Astra's STR/SAR filing runbook walks through the goAML submission process end-to-end.

The UAE Local Terrorist List​

This is a CBUAE-mandatory sanctions list issued by the Executive Office of the Committee for Goods and Materials Subjected to Import and Export Control. It is not contained in OpenSanctions — Shield Astra customers must obtain it separately from the CBUAE portal and load it as a custom list.

Failing to screen against the UAE Local Terrorist List would be a finding in a CBUAE inspection. See Continuous Screening › Regulator pack for the integration procedure.

What this means for the reader​

Two takeaways from this page:

  1. Every scenario, rule, and screening feature in Shield Astra cites at least one framework above. When you read Rule Library › Fiat › Sanctions you will see every rule's citation on the rule's own line. This is deliberate — it's what an auditor asks for, and it makes tuning defensible when compliance meets engineering.

  2. The product does not do all your compliance work. Shield Astra detects, alerts, records, and files. The licensee's compliance function still owns:

    • Setting the rule thresholds to the risk appetite
    • Reviewing every non-Approve case within the SLA
    • Deciding what constitutes an STR/SAR
    • Board and regulator communication
    • The vendor risk register including Shield Astra itself
