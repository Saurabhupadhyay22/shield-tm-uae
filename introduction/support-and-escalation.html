Support and escalation

During UAT / Pilot — support runs through your account manager

The channels listed on this page describe the commercial-support model that applies once your Master Services Agreement (MSA) is signed and support mailboxes go live. You are currently in a UAT / pilot phase. For every question, incident, or licensing conversation, contact your the vendor account manager directly — they route to the right person internally. This page is kept for reference so you can see the model that will apply post-launch.

Shield Astra is licensed software, and The vendor provides commercial support under the Master Services Agreement (MSA) attached to every release. This page tells you which channel to use and how fast to expect a response for each situation.

Severity bands (per MSA §7 SLA)​

Severity is measured by customer impact, not by our judgement of "technical severity."

SeverityDefinitionTarget first responseTarget resolution
Sev 1 — CriticalProduction Shield Astra unavailable or unusable for the licensee; no workaround1 hour4 hours (workaround), 24 hours (fix)
Sev 2 — HighMajor functionality degraded; workaround exists but is disruptive4 hours24 hours (workaround), 72 hours (fix)
Sev 3 — MediumIsolated defect or single feature affected; workaround acceptable1 business dayNext scheduled release
Sev 4 — Low / questionCosmetic issue, docs question, feature request2 business daysAs appropriate; no SLA on fixes

The Enterprise support tier upgrades Sev 1 and Sev 2 to 24×7 coverage. See your MSA §7 for the exact SLA that applies to your contract.

Before you contact support​

For fastest resolution, gather these before you open the ticket:

For a production incident:

  • Shield Astra version (docker exec omnis-backend cat /app/VERSION)
  • Symptom in one sentence
  • Timestamp (UTC) when it started
  • Last 500 lines of backend log (docker logs --tail 500 omnis-backend)
  • Whether it started after a config change, upgrade, or on its own
  • Whether it's affecting one customer / one scenario / everything

For a support question:

  • What you are trying to accomplish (job to be done, not the specific command that isn't working)
  • What you've already tried
  • Screenshot of the relevant UI, if UI-related
  • Relevant rule / scenario / case ID

Escalation ladder​

If you don't hear back within the SLA target, or the response isn't landing:

  1. Reply to the original support thread with the phrase "Escalating for management review"
  2. If still no movement within a further business day, email escalations@solutio.ae
  3. For a stuck production incident, phone the on-call number in your MSA §7

Vendor leadership is copied on every step of the escalation ladder.

What we can and cannot do during a regulator interaction​

The vendor is a licensed-software vendor to your business, not your regulator's counterparty. In practical terms:

  • We can attend a CBUAE or VARA meeting with you as a vendor representative, on request
  • We can supply the release SBOM, DPIA, and vendor-audit pack the regulator asks for
  • We can walk the regulator through the product's architecture, controls, and audit trail
  • We cannot answer for the licensee's compliance decisions (rule thresholds you set, cases you closed as no-action, STRs you did or didn't file)
  • We cannot access your production data without a written, per-incident, time-limited authorisation from you (per DPA §5)
