What is Shield Astra

Shield Astra is a transaction monitoring and anti-money-laundering (AML) compliance product for financial institutions. This documentation covers Shield Astra and its purpose-built white-labelled deployment for the UAE market.

Shield Astra and Omnis TM

Omnis TM is the white-labelled Shield Astra deployment shipped to UAE Payment Services Providers and Virtual Asset Service Providers. The two products share the same code, the same data model, the same rule engine, and the same case-manager surface. Everything documented on this site applies equally to Shield Astra installations and to Omnis TM installations. Where a UAE-specific compliance requirement matters, the Compliance (UAE) chapter calls it out explicitly.

Shield Astra is designed for two specific licensed populations in the UAE:

  • Payment Services Providers (PSPs) regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) under the Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes Regulation
  • Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) regulated by the Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (VARA) in Dubai, or by ADGM, DFSA, or FSRA in the other Emirates

Shield Astra is delivered as a signed, self-hosted software package. The licensee runs it on their own Azure UAE North (or on-prem UAE) infrastructure. The vendor's role is the software vendor — comparable to how Napier, ComplyAdvantage, NICE Actimize, and Feedzai ship AML platforms to regulated banks — not the data controller and not the operator.

What Shield Astra does​

At a glance, Shield Astra covers the seven daily jobs of a UAE PSP or VASP compliance team:

Detect suspicious transactions in real timeScenarios + rules engine
Automate common risk decisionsScore-based decisioning (Approve / Review / Block / Decline)
Route alerts to a compliance analystCase Manager with inboxes and workflows
Screen customers against sanctions and PEP listsContinuous Screening (Yente + OpenSanctions)
Perform enhanced due diligence on high-risk casesKYC Enrichment + AI Case Review
File STR/SAR reports to the UAE FIUgoAML export with audit trail
Prove compliance to CBUAE and VARA inspectors5-year audit trail, immutable decision log, SBOM, DPIA

What it ships with, out of the box​

Shield Astra is not a blank framework. It ships pre-built with:

  • 108 industry-standard AML rules covering fiat (66 rules) and crypto (42 rules) — with regulatory citations to FATF, CBUAE, VARA, and UAE goAML for every rule. See the Rule Library chapter.
  • 8-table data model matching how vendors like Napier and ComplyAdvantage structure their customer + transaction records — one row per customer, one row per account, one row per transaction, plus screening signals. See the Data Model chapter.
  • AI Case Review generator that produces narrative case reviews in the compliance officer's preferred structure, using either OpenAI or a drop-in alternative (Azure OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Vertex, or a self-hosted open model). See the AI Case Review page.
  • Continuous Screening against OpenSanctions (OFAC, EU, UN, HMT, and 464 other sanctions and PEP datasets), auto-refreshing on a configurable schedule. See the Continuous Screening chapter.
  • UAE-specific compliance runbooks for STR/SAR filing, MLRO escalation, CBUAE inspection preparation, and audit trail export. See the Compliance Runbooks chapter.

What Shield Astra is not​

Being clear about what the product deliberately does not do saves the reader time:

  • Not a KYC onboarding product. Shield Astra presumes your customers have already been onboarded and KYC'd by another system (or vendor: Sumsub, Onfido, Jumio, etc.). It monitors transactions and the customer's ongoing risk profile after onboarding.
  • Not a card-issuance or ledger system. Money movement is upstream — Shield Astra only sees the finished transaction event.
  • Not a fraud-authorisation product. For payment fraud in the authorisation window (< 200 ms decisioning on a card swipe), Shield Astra's rule engine is not the right tool. Use a specialised fraud-scoring vendor (Feedzai, Featurespace, Sardine) for that surface. Shield Astra handles the AML dimension — structuring, layering, velocity, sanctions — which is measured in seconds to hours, not milliseconds.
  • Not a chain analytics product. For crypto counterparty risk scores, Shield Astra reads scores you supply from Chainalysis KYT, TRM Labs, or Elliptic — it does not compute them itself.

How Shield Astra is delivered​

Shield Astra is delivered as a signed, single-tenant deployment that runs on the licensee's own infrastructure — typically Azure UAE North, but any UAE-based on-prem VM works. Every release ships with a Software Bill of Materials (SBOM), a Master Services Agreement (MSA), a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), and the full sub-processor register the licensee shows to CBUAE / VARA. Every third-party component the product depends on is documented in the Vendors & Integrations chapter.
