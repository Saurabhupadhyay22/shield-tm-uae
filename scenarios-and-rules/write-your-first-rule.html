Write your first rule

Goal​

Add a rule to the Fiat Transaction Monitoring scenario that says:

If the transaction amount is over AED 100,000 AND the customer's KYC status is not VERIFIED, add 15 points.

You'll do it entirely in the visual builder — no SQL, no code.

Time: 8 minutes.

Prerequisites​

  • You've done Send your first transaction (or your product owner has)
  • You have the Analyst or MLRO role — see Administration → Users and roles
  • The Fiat TM scenario is Live (it ships Live by default)
The live scenario stays live

You never edit the live iteration in place. You duplicate it, edit the copy, test the copy, then submit for MLRO approval. Steps 1–3 below create the copy — nothing changes for real transactions until Step 8.

Step 1 — Open the Fiat TM scenario​

Left sidebar → Scenarios → click Fiat Transaction Monitoring.

Step 2 — Create a new draft iteration​

On the scenario detail page, click + New draft in the top-right. This clones the current V9 Live into V10 as a Draft.

Step 3 — Go to the Rules tab of the draft​

Click Rules at the top of the draft page. You land on the full rules list — 66 rules for the Fiat TM scenario, each with its name, description, group, and score modifier.

Step 4 — Name the rule and set the score​

The visual builder opens. Fill in:

FieldValue
NameLarge transfer + KYC not verified
DescriptionFires when amount > AED 100,000 and the customer's KYC is not VERIFIED. FATF R.10 + CBUAE §3.
Rule groupCustomer profile & PEP (pick from the dropdown)
Score modifier+15

Save the draft rule. It's now in the iteration but doesn't fire yet until you add a condition.

Step 5 — Add the first condition (amount)​

Click + Add condition. The condition editor opens:

  1. Left operand: transaction.amount_aed (the field picker walks the data model — see Create links between tables)
  2. Operator: >
  3. Right operand: 100000 (typed literal)

Save. The rule now says "fires if transaction.amount_aed > 100000".

Step 6 — Add the second condition (KYC)​

Click + Add AND condition. Second condition:

  1. Left operand: transaction.customer.kyc_status (walks the fiat_transfer → customer link)
  2. Operator: !=
  3. Right operand: "VERIFIED" (typed string literal)

Save. The rule now reads:

transaction.amount_aed > 100000
    AND transaction.customer.kyc_status != "VERIFIED"
→ +15

Step 7 — Test the rule against sample data​

In the rule editor sidebar, click Test with sample data. Paste a fiat_transfer payload — e.g. the one from Send your first transaction.

Two outcomes:

  • Rule fires
  • Rule doesn't fire

You see Fired — +15 points. Your rule is correctly wired to the data model. Move to Step 8.

The trace panel shows which condition failed. Common causes:

  • transaction.customer.kyc_status is NULL — the customer's payload didn't include this field. Update the customer or your test payload.
  • The amount was under 100,000 — increase the sample.
  • The customer link doesn't resolve — the customer_id on the transaction doesn't match any customer. Ingest the customer first.

Step 8 — Save and preview the whole draft​

Click Save rule. Back on the Rules tab, your new rule appears in the list — search for its name to find it fast.

Before submitting for approval, click Replay against history → Last 24h. Shield Astra re-runs every past 24-hour decision as if this draft were live and shows you:

  • Cases added — transactions that would newly escalate
  • Cases removed — transactions that would newly clear
  • FP-rate delta — estimated false-positive impact

See Duplicating for tuning for the deep dive.

Step 9 — Submit for MLRO approval​

Top-right of the draft page → Submit for approval. Enter a change reason ("Adds a customer-profile rule requested by MLRO after 2026-Q3 review"). Submit.

The MLRO reviews and approves the draft — see Lifecycle. On approval, V9 becomes archived and V10 becomes Live. Your rule starts firing on the very next transaction ingested.

What just happened​

  • You created a new iteration V10 without touching V9 Live
  • You added a rule with two conditions on linked fields
  • You tested it against sample + historical data
  • V9 stays live until MLRO approves V10
